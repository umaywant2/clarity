## **1. The Canon as a World**

The TriadicFrameworks Canon is a structured landscape composed of five major territories:

1. **Triadic Territories** — Structure, Resonance, Activation  
2. **Substrate Fields** — primitives, pulses, spectral actions  
3. **Evaluator Provinces** — Drift, Coherence, Regime, Clarity  
4. **Analyzer Layer Regions** — operator, dimensional, drift, coherence, regime, crosscut  
5. **Module Domains** — engines, profiles, signatures, diagnostics, maps, templates  

The Canon Atlas charts these territories and shows how they interconnect.

---

## **2. Triadic Territories**

### **Structure Territory (S)**  
A land of form, constraint, axis, and stability.  
Mountains represent fixed forms; rivers represent axes; plains represent stable envelopes.

### **Resonance Territory (R)**  
A harmonic landscape of fields, relations, and coherence.  
Valleys echo harmonic pulses; networks of paths represent relational flows.

### **Activation Territory (A)**  
A dynamic region of impulse, motion, and surge.  
Storm fronts represent activation surges; shifting dunes represent volatility.

These three territories form the **Triadic Continent** — the foundation of the entire canon.

---

## **3. Substrate Fields**

The Substrate is the underlying “soil” of the Triadic Continent.

### **Oscillation Fields**  
Rolling harmonic plains; periodic waves across the land.

### **PhaseShift Faultlines**  
Tectonic drift boundaries; regions where displacement occurs.

### **Harmonic Basins**  
Stable resonance pools; coherence centers.

### **LineagePulse Currents**  
Deep underground currents tracing qmroot → triad → layer → operator.

These fields feed every evaluator and every module.

---

## **4. Evaluator Provinces**

The Evaluators are four major provinces arranged in a clockwise ring:

### **Drift Province**  
Characterized by ΔS/ΔR/ΔA winds, drift vectors, and oscillation cycles.

### **Coherence Province**  
A region of harmonic envelopes, variance valleys, and stability ridges.

### **Regime Province**  
Dominance plateaus: structural, resonance, and activation regimes.

### **Clarity Province**  
Pulse forests, spectral springs, and clarity peaks.

At the center of the ring lies the **Session Interpreter Capital**, where all four provinces converge.

---

## **5. Analyzer Layer Regions**

Surrounding the Evaluator Provinces are six major regions:

### **Operator Region**  
Spectral operators, drift operators, regime operators, primitive operators.

### **Dimensional Region**  
D0–D7 dimensional families arranged as ascending terraces.

### **Drift Region**  
Gradual, sudden, oscillating, and paradox drift zones.

### **Coherence Region**  
High, medium, and low coherence belts.

### **Regime Region**  
Structural, resonance, and activation dominance districts.

### **Crosscut Region**  
Lineage roads, envelope bridges, stability towers, compatibility gates.

These regions form the **Analyzer Ring** around the Evaluator Provinces.

---

## **6. Module Domains**

Beyond the Analyzer Ring lie the Module Domains — the outer world of the canon.

### **Engine Domain**  
Home of the Drift, Coherence, Regime, Clarity, and Session engines.

### **Profile Domain**  
Biomes representing module profiles and identity signatures.

### **Signature Domain**  
Glyph forests; symbolic representations of module signatures.

### **Diagnostic Domain**  
Observation towers; drift/coherence/regime/clarity diagnostic stations.

### **Map Domain**  
Cartographic centers; module maps and lineage charts.

### **Template Domain**  
Blueprint libraries; module.json templates and metadata structures.

These domains are where modules live, evolve, and interoperate.

## **7. TRO Landscape**

The **Triadic Response Object (TRO)** is the “weather system” of the canon:

- triadic winds  
- substrate currents  
- evaluator storms

The **SessionTRO** is the full climate model of the entire continent.
