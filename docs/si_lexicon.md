# **Structural Intelligence — Lexicon (SI Lexicon)**  
### Canon Version: R5  
### File: `/docs/si_lexicon.md`

---

## **1. Purpose**

The **SI Lexicon** defines the official vocabulary of Structural Intelligence.  
Each entry provides a concise, academically‑aligned definition suitable for:

- SI Publications  
- SI Knowledge Base  
- SI Ontology  
- RTT Suite evaluators  
- module.json metadata  
- Semantic API endpoints  

This lexicon is the authoritative dictionary of SI terminology.

---

## **2. Lexicon Format**

Each entry includes:

- **Term** — canonical name  
- **Domain** — Triadic / Substrate / Evaluator / Metadata / Module / Pipeline / Canon / TRO  
- **Definition** — concise, deterministic meaning  
- **Notes** — optional clarifications  

---

## **3. Lexicon Entries**

---

### **Activation (A)**  
**Domain:** Triadic  
**Definition:** The triadic axis of impulse, motion, and surge; represents change, volatility, and directional force.

---

### **Analyzer Layer**  
**Domain:** Metadata  
**Definition:** One of six canonical metadata layers (operator, dimensional, drift, coherence, regime, crosscut) that define evaluator behavior and module lineage.

---

### **Clarity**  
**Domain:** Evaluator  
**Definition:** Evaluator measuring transparency, pulse signatures, and spectral clarity within triadic content.

---

### **Coherence**  
**Domain:** Evaluator  
**Definition:** Evaluator measuring alignment, harmonic envelope, and relational stability.

---

### **Coupling**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Degree of interaction between substrate primitives; determines resonance strength and stability.

---

### **Delta (Δ‑family)**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Substrate primitives representing directional change; includes ΔS, ΔR, ΔA.

---

### **Dimensional Layer**  
**Domain:** Metadata  
**Definition:** Analyzer layer defining D0–D7 dimensional families used for structural classification.

---

### **Drift**  
**Domain:** Evaluator  
**Definition:** Evaluator measuring ΔS/ΔR/ΔA behavior, displacement, and structural change.

---

### **Evaluator**  
**Domain:** Evaluator  
**Definition:** One of the four RTT evaluators (Drift, Coherence, Regime, Clarity) or the Session Interpreter.

---

### **Harmonic**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Substrate primitive representing resonance stability and harmonic alignment.

---

### **Lineage**  
**Domain:** Metadata  
**Definition:** Canonical chain: qmroot → triad → layer → operator → evaluator.

---

### **LineagePulse**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Primitive representing lineage traversal; used in crosscut analysis.

---

### **Module**  
**Domain:** Module  
**Definition:** Canon entity defined by module.json; includes identity, metadata, lineage, analyzer layers, roles, and session context.

---

### **Op‑family**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Substrate primitives representing triadic actions; includes oscillation, phaseShift, harmonic.

---

### **Oscillation**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Harmonic motion primitive; periodic triadic behavior.

---

### **Polarity (SoN ↔ NoS)**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Binary substrate orientation representing structural vs. non‑structural alignment.

---

### **Regime**  
**Domain:** Evaluator  
**Definition:** Evaluator measuring operating mode dominance (Rg.S, Rg.R, Rg.A).

---

### **Resonance (R)**  
**Domain:** Triadic  
**Definition:** The triadic axis of relation, harmonic, and field; represents alignment and coherence.

---

### **Rg‑family**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Substrate primitives representing operating modes; includes structural, resonance, and activation regimes.

---

### **Session Interpreter**  
**Domain:** Evaluator  
**Definition:** Full RTT pipeline evaluator running Drift → Coherence → Regime → Clarity sequentially.

---

### **Stability Envelope**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Substrate stability classification (low, medium, high).

---

### **Structure (S)**  
**Domain:** Triadic  
**Definition:** The triadic axis of form, constraint, axis, and stability.

---

### **Substrate**  
**Domain:** Substrate  
**Definition:** Fundamental layer of SI containing Δ/Op/Rg primitives, polarity, stability, coupling, and resonance.

---

### **TRO (Triadic Response Object)**  
**Domain:** TRO  
**Definition:** Canonical output format of RTT evaluators; contains triadic, substrate, evaluator, commentary, and metadata fields.

---

### **Triadic**  
**Domain:** Triadic  
**Definition:** The three‑axis structure (S/R/A) underlying all SI concepts, evaluators, and modules.

---

### **Triadic Map**  
**Domain:** Semantic API  
**Definition:** Semantic API endpoint mapping text or TRO into triadic structure and semantic graph.

---

### **Triadic Orthogonality**  
**Domain:** Canon  
**Definition:** Canon constraint requiring S, R, A to remain orthogonal and non‑collapsing.

---

### **Variance**  
**Domain:** Evaluator  
**Definition:** Coherence metric measuring relational spread and harmonic deviation.

---
