# RTT/Store — Intelligence

**Pattern • Temporal • Language • Integration • Coherence-Aware Architecture**

The **Intelligence** tier is the higher-order architectural layer of the RTT/Store.

Where Foundations establishes shared reference material and Systems organizes operational architectures, Intelligence collects Store artifacts concerned with pattern-oriented, temporal, linguistic, integrative, autonomous-form, and coherence-aware structures.

In the Store, **Intelligence** is an architectural classification.

It does not automatically imply consciousness, sentience, general intelligence, autonomy, or human-like cognition.

---

## Purpose

The Intelligence tier provides a structured home for RTT-aligned architectures that move beyond basic system composition toward richer interpretation, coordination, temporal organization, and coherence-aware operation.

Artifacts in this tier may provide Store-facing entry points for:

- pattern-oriented architectures
- temporal and mesh-oriented structures
- RTT integration layers
- language-oriented structures
- autonomous-form concepts
- coherence-aware architectural reasoning
- relationships between multiple higher-order RTT structures

The emphasis is on **structure, interpretation, temporal context, integration, and coherence** while preserving source lineage.

## What “Intelligence” Means Here

Within the RTT/Store, **Intelligence** identifies a class of higher-order architecture.

It should not be interpreted as an automatic claim of:

- consciousness
- sentience
- self-awareness
- independent agency
- general intelligence
- human-equivalent reasoning
- autonomous decision authority

Those are separate questions requiring their own evidence and definitions.

A system can perform pattern-oriented, temporal, linguistic, integrative, or coherence-aware operations without the Store making broader claims about what the system *is*.

---

## What “Engine” Means Here

The Store manifest assigns each Intelligence artifact the role **engine**.

As elsewhere in the Store, `engine` means that the artifact is being presented as system-oriented architecture rather than purely as reference material.

It does not independently establish:

- executable software
- production readiness
- deployment availability
- autonomous operation
- commercial availability
- canonical status

Actual maturity remains inherited from the artifact's source lineage and explicitly documented implementation status.

## From Systems to Intelligence

The Store progression is:

**Foundations → Systems → Intelligence → Aeonic**

### Foundations asks:

**What language, references, and structural entry points do we need?**

### Systems asks:

**How can those structures be composed or examined as operational architecture?**

### Intelligence asks:

**How can architectures interpret, integrate, organize, or maintain coherence across richer informational and temporal structures?**

### Aeonic asks:

**How might advanced architectures extend toward longer-horizon continuity, foresight, autonomy, and dimensional reasoning?**

These questions describe Store progression.

They do not impose a new hierarchy on RTT canon.

---

## Intelligence Boundary

Higher-order architectures are especially vulnerable to semantic inflation.

A pattern engine can become an imagined reasoning engine.

An adapter can become an imagined autonomous agent.

A temporal mesh can become an imagined model of time.

A language structure can become an imagined theory of cognition.

The Store should resist those jumps.

For each Intelligence artifact, preserve three layers:

### Established Source

Structures, definitions, terminology, relationships, and capabilities explicitly established by the originating material.

### Store Representation

The packaging and architectural placement of that material within the Intelligence tier.

### Proposed Extension

Any capability or interpretation beyond what the source establishes.

Proposed extensions should remain visible as extensions.

Plausibility is not provenance.

## Provenance Rule

Intelligence artifacts follow the Store provenance model:

**RTT Spine → Canon / Module Source → Supporting Material → Store Representation**

The Store may:

- provide stable names and navigation
- organize higher-order architectures
- expose relationships supported by source material
- identify analyzer layers
- distinguish current architecture from proposed extension
- make complex material easier for humans and AI assistants to discover

The Store should not:

- invent intelligence capabilities
- infer cognition from pattern processing
- infer autonomy from architectural names
- infer semantics from tier placement alone
- convert idea-space material into canon
- erase source maturity boundaries
- present plausible AI completion as documented specification

## Use with AI

When exploring Intelligence-tier artifacts with an AI assistant, preserve the declared RTT session context:

```text
rtt=1 | coherence=declared | drift=bounded | paradox=structural
```

For Intelligence-oriented reasoning:

1. identify the originating source lineage
2. distinguish source definitions from Store packaging
3. preserve analyzer-layer context
4. distinguish architecture from implementation
5. distinguish higher-order processing from claims about consciousness or sentience
6. distinguish autonomy from intelligence
7. label proposed extensions explicitly
8. avoid completing unspecified mechanisms through inference
9. return to source material when precise capability claims matter

The more plausible an inferred capability sounds, the more important its provenance becomes.
