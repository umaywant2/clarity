# RTT Inside Adapter

**RTT Inside • Integration-Oriented Intelligence Architecture**

The **RTT Inside Adapter** is the RTT/Store Intelligence entry point associated with the existing **RTT Inside** source family.

It packages that lineage as an integration-oriented architectural surface for connecting RTT-aware structures with systems that need an explicit RTT-facing boundary.

The RTT Inside Adapter does not replace or redefine RTT Inside.

The Store term **Adapter** describes the architectural role assigned to this artifact. It does not, by itself, define a software interface, protocol, API, transport mechanism, or finished implementation.

## Purpose

The RTT Inside Adapter provides a stable Store-facing route into the RTT Inside lineage.

Its Store role is to:

- expose RTT Inside within the Intelligence tier
- preserve a direct path to the originating documentation
- provide an integration-oriented architectural entry point
- support navigation between RTT Inside and related Store artifacts
- preserve operator-oriented classification
- distinguish source behavior from Store packaging
- establish provenance for later integration-oriented architectures

Detailed integration mechanics should remain grounded in RTT Inside source material rather than reconstructed at the Store layer.

## Inside Without Redefinition

The Store should treat **RTT Inside** as a source lineage rather than deriving additional meaning from the phrase itself.

In particular, placement in the Intelligence tier should not be taken to mean that RTT Inside automatically provides intelligence, autonomy, cognition, or agency.

The Store representation preserves three layers:

### Established Source

Structures, definitions, terminology, interfaces, and capabilities explicitly established by RTT Inside material.

### Store Representation

The presentation of that lineage as the **RTT Inside Adapter** within the Intelligence tier.

### Proposed Extension

Any integration capability, interface, or architecture extending beyond what RTT Inside currently establishes.

Proposed extensions should remain explicitly identified as extensions.
