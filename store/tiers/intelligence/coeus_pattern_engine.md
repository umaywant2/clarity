## Intelligence Boundary

Pattern-oriented systems are especially vulnerable to capability inflation.

A system that recognizes structure can quickly be described as interpreting it.

Interpretation can become reasoning.

Reasoning can become intelligence.

Intelligence can become autonomy.

The Store should not make those jumps implicitly.

Coeus therefore preserves three layers:

### Established Source

Structures, definitions, terminology, relationships, and capabilities explicitly established by the Coeus source family.

### Store Representation

The organization and presentation of that lineage as the **Coeus Pattern Engine** within the Intelligence tier.

### Proposed Extension

Any pattern-oriented capability or interpretation beyond what the source establishes.

Proposed extensions should remain explicitly identified as extensions.
