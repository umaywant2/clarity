# TLS Tier1

**Triadic Language Stack • Coherence-Oriented Intelligence Architecture**

The **TLS Tier1** artifact is the RTT/Store Intelligence entry point associated with the existing **Triadic Language Stack (TLS)** source lineage.

It packages TLS material as a language-oriented, coherence-aware Intelligence architecture for discovery, navigation, and structured exploration.

TLS Tier1 does not replace or redefine the Triadic Language Stack.

The Store preserves the established `Tier1` identity without independently assigning semantics, capabilities, or maturity to that tier beyond what the originating TLS material establishes.

## Purpose

TLS Tier1 provides a stable Intelligence-tier route into Triadic Language Stack material.

Its Store role is to:

- expose TLS within the Intelligence tier
- preserve a direct path to its originating source
- provide a language-oriented architectural entry point
- preserve coherence as the primary Store analysis lens
- support navigation between TLS and related RTT artifacts
