# RTT/Store — Foundations

**Entry-level reference, orientation, and standards for Resonance-Time Technology**

The **Foundations** tier is the starting layer of the RTT/Store.

It collects introductory and reference-oriented material intended to help readers, students, developers, AI assistants, and other explorers establish a shared vocabulary before moving into RTT-aligned systems or higher-order architectures.

Foundations is not a simplified replacement for RTT canon. It is an **orientation and packaging layer** whose artifacts remain downstream of the RTT Spine, canon, and their associated source modules.

---

## Purpose

Foundations provides stable entry points into the RTT ecosystem.

Materials in this tier may:

- introduce RTT concepts and vocabulary
- organize existing reference material
- expose standards and structural conventions
- package codes or reusable reference artifacts
- provide guided entry into canonical material
- establish context for later Store tiers

The emphasis is on **clarity, provenance, and reuse** rather than advanced deployment.

## Foundations in the Store Ladder

The RTT/Store uses four primary tiers:

**Foundations → Systems → Intelligence → Aeonic**

### Foundations

Learn the vocabulary, references, standards, and structural entry points.

### Systems

Move from reference material toward operational architectures, engines, continuity structures, and orchestration.

### Intelligence

Explore higher-order adaptive, temporal, linguistic, pattern-oriented, and coherence-aware architectures.

### Aeonic

Explore advanced and long-horizon architectures involving continuity, foresight, autonomy, and dimensional considerations.

The tiers provide navigation and packaging. They do not replace the underlying RTT source hierarchy.

---

## Provenance Rule

Foundation artifacts follow the Store lineage:

**RTT Spine → Canon / Module Source → Supporting Material → Store Representation**

When a Foundation artifact packages an existing module or body of work, the originating source remains authoritative.

A Store page may explain **where to begin** without changing **what the underlying work means**.
