# Continuity Engine

**Continuity Kernel • Coherence-Oriented Systems Architecture**

The **Continuity Engine** is the RTT/Store Systems entry point associated with the existing **Continuity Kernel** lineage.

It packages Continuity Kernel material as a system-facing architecture for discovery, architectural reasoning, and connection to other RTT/Store artifacts.

The Continuity Engine does not replace or redefine the Continuity Kernel.

The Store name **Continuity Engine** identifies its role within the Systems tier. It does not, by itself, imply a finished software implementation, deployed service, or commercially available product.

## Purpose

The Continuity Engine provides a stable Systems-level route into the Continuity Kernel lineage.

Its Store role is to:

- expose continuity-oriented material within the Systems tier
- preserve a direct path to its canonical source
- provide a common architectural reference for continuity-related Store artifacts
- support system-level reasoning without duplicating the Kernel
- distinguish established source material from Store packaging
- provide a lineage anchor for later long-horizon and advanced continuity architectures

Detailed Continuity Kernel mechanics should be taken from the originating source rather than reconstructed at the Store layer.
