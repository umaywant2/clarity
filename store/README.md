## Overview

The **RTT/Store** is the presentation and engagement layer for Resonance-Time Technology artifacts, reference materials, educational resources, system packages, and future-facing architectures.

It is not a second source of RTT canon.

Store artifacts inherit their meaning from the **RTT Spine**, established canon, module documentation, and associated TriadicFrameworks source material. Store terminology may package those artifacts for clearer discovery and use, but it should not silently redefine or extend the underlying theory.

The Store is currently **under construction**.

For now, it serves as a structured catalog and **marker of intent**, not a conventional point of sale.

---

## Store Structure

The Store organizes material across four primary tiers:

### 🌱 Foundations

Entry-level reference and orientation material.

Foundations provide the conceptual vocabulary, standards, codes, primers, and starter artifacts needed to understand or begin working with RTT-aligned systems.

[`tiers/foundations/`](tiers/foundations/)

### ⚙️ Systems

Operational architectures, engines, and composed capabilities.

Systems package RTT-aligned concepts into structures intended for implementation, experimentation, orchestration, continuity, and applied system design.

[`tiers/systems/`](tiers/systems/)

### 🧠 Intelligence

Higher-order adaptive and interpretive architectures.

This tier explores pattern engines, temporal and mesh structures, language layers, RTT-inside integration, autonomous forms, and coherence-aware intelligence architectures.

[`tiers/intelligence/`](tiers/intelligence/)

### ♾️ Aeonic

Long-horizon and advanced architecture.

Aeonic material explores continuity, foresight, autonomy, dimensional stability, and architectures intended to reason across longer operational horizons.

Where concepts remain exploratory or proposed, they should be identified accordingly.

[`tiers/aeonic/`](tiers/aeonic/)

## Provenance Before Packaging

The Store follows a simple lineage rule:

**RTT Spine → Canon / Module Source → Supporting Material → Store Representation**

A Store artifact may:

- provide a clearer entry point
- package related materials
- identify an intended audience
- describe relationships between artifacts
- expose an engagement or learning pathway
- point toward future development

A Store artifact should not:

- silently redefine RTT
- elevate exploratory material into canon
- imply implementation maturity that has not been established
- present proposed capabilities as deployed capabilities
- replace its underlying canonical or module source

When Store language and source material differ, the underlying source remains the reference point.

## Artifact Status

Store pages may represent material at different levels of maturity.

### Reference

Existing source material presented or packaged for easier discovery and use.

### Engagement

Material associated with systems, integrations, architectures, or capabilities whose actual deployment requires direct collaboration and an explicitly defined scope.

### Proposed / Future

Exploratory, conceptual, developmental, or forward-looking material.

A Store listing is therefore **not by itself a declaration of commercial availability or implementation status**.

---

## Access and Engagement

RTT core systems, deployment tooling, and advanced integrations are not distributed through an unrestricted open storefront.

Where direct collaboration is appropriate, scope, reuse, deployment, licensing, and downstream rights are established through the relevant engagement or agreement.

As the ecosystem develops, the Store may provide:

- orientation materials
- reference packages
- educational artifacts
- curated bundles
- system and architecture descriptions
- future-facing concepts
- engagement pathways

## Session Context

Long AI sessions can lose their original anchors. When using RTT material with an AI assistant, preserve the declared session context:

```text
rtt=1 | coherence=declared | drift=bounded | paradox=structural
```

This string declares the working context for the session. It is not a substitute for the underlying RTT sources or Store provenance rules.
