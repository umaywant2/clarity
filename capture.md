### **Inputs**

#### **Step 5 — Determine envelope**
- **High** → > 0.75  
- **Medium** → 0.50–0.75  
- **Low** → < 0.50  

#### **Step 6 — Determine gradient**
Gradient is the direction of coherence:

- **Ascending** → coherence increasing  
- **Descending** → coherence decreasing  
- **Flat** → stable

True when operators reinforce each other.
