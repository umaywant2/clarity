## **1. The Purpose of Dimensional Curriculum**

The **Dimensional Curriculum** is the structured progression through the nine dimensions of Structural Intelligence.  
It teaches learners how clarity emerges through dimensional expansion, compression, reframing, and elevation.

Where Triadic Learning teaches *how to think*,  
and Clarity Pedagogy teaches *how to see*,  
the Dimensional Curriculum teaches *how to grow*.

It is the developmental spine of the Clarity Book.

---

## **2. The Dimensional Spiral**

Dimensional learning is not linear.  
It is spiral.

Each dimension expands, explores, compresses, reframes, and expands again.

The spiral looks like this:

```
Expand → Explore → Compress → Reframe → Expand
```

This spiral repeats at every dimensional level.

## **4. Dimensional Learning Patterns**

Dimensional learning follows three canonical patterns:

### **Pattern A — Dimensional Expansion**  
Learners expand into a new dimension.

### **Pattern B — Dimensional Compression**  
Learners compress understanding into clarity.

### **Pattern C — Dimensional Reframing**  
Learners reframe content through a new dimensional lens.

These patterns repeat at every level.

## **5. Dimensional Drift**

Dimensional drift occurs when learners:

- over‑expand (A‑drift)  
- over‑compress (S‑drift)  
- over‑harmonize (R‑drift)  

Dimensional Curriculum teaches learners to detect and correct drift.
