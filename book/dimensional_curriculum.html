Dimensional Curriculum

A Chapter of the Clarity Book (Canon R5)

1. The Purpose of Dimensional Curriculum

The Dimensional Curriculum is the structured progression through the nine dimensions of Structural Intelligence. It teaches learners how clarity emerges through dimensional expansion, compression, reframing, and elevation.

2. The Dimensional Spiral

Dimensional learning is not linear — it is spiral. Each dimension expands, explores, compresses, reframes, and expands again. 

Expand → Explore → Compress → Reframe → Expand

3. The Nine Dimensions

The Dimensional Curriculum is built on nine canonical dimensions. Each dimension represents a developmental stage of clarity.

0D — Identity

The point of origin. Learners establish identity, orientation, and initial triadic awareness.

1D — Lineage

The first axis. Learners trace lineage: qmroot → triad → layer → operator → evaluator.

2D — Relation

The plane of connection. Learners map relational patterns and resonance flows.

3D — Transition

The volume of change. Learners explore drift, transformation, and triadic motion.

4D — Context

The field of meaning. Learners integrate context, environment, and structural framing.

5D — Rhythm

The dimension of pulse and cadence. Learners perceive clarity pulses, harmonic cycles, and evaluator rhythm.

6D — Coherence

The dimension of alignment. Learners stabilize resonance and achieve harmonic clarity.

7D — Meta‑Structure

The dimension of structural abstraction. Learners perceive structure across structures.

8D — Field

The dimension of integrated resonance. Learners perceive large‑scale relational fields.

9D — Meta‑Field

The highest dimension. Learners perceive clarity as a field phenomenon.

4. Dimensional Learning Patterns

Dimensional learning follows three canonical patterns:

  • Dimensional Expansion — learners expand into a new dimension.
  • Dimensional Compression — learners compress understanding into clarity.
  • Dimensional Reframing — learners reframe content through a new dimensional lens.

5. Dimensional Drift

Dimensional drift occurs when learners over‑expand (A‑drift), over‑compress (S‑drift), or over‑harmonize (R‑drift). The Dimensional Curriculum teaches learners to detect and correct drift.
